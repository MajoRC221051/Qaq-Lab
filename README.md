# Q’AQ’ Lab

**Know when NASA evidence applies to your experiment.**

Q’AQ’ Lab helps research students connect a proposed combustion experiment with experimental evidence already available from NASA.

Students enter conditions such as material, geometry, oxygen level, pressure, airflow, and gravity. Q’AQ’ Lab retrieves comparable NASA tests, shows which conditions match or differ, and provides the documented outcome with a link to the original report and page.

When the available evidence does not cover the proposed conditions, Q’AQ’ Lab makes that limitation visible and identifies the next experiment needed instead of generating an unsupported conclusion.
